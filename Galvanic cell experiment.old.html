Interactive galvanic cell experiment: choose any two metal electrodes from magnesium, aluminium, zinc, iron, lead, copper, silver and gold for the left and right half-cells, and read the resulting emf on an analogue voltmeter rescaled to 0 to 4 volts. If the electrodes are wired with the more positive one on the left, the digital readout shows a negative voltage and the analogue needle strains just past zero, pegging near its stop, prompting the electrodes to be swapped.

Zn | Zn²⁺ (1.00 M) || Cu²⁺ (1.00 M) | Cu
Circuit open
Digital check: 0.00 V

Left electrode — black terminal

Oxidation (anode)

Zn(s) → Zn²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻

E° = -0.76 V

Right electrode — red terminal

Reduction (cathode)

Cu²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻ → Cu(s)

E° = +0.34 V

E°cell = E°cathode - E°anode = 0.34 - (-0.76) = 1.10 V. Both ions are at 1.00 M, so Q = 1 and the Nernst correction is zero — E = E°. The salt bridge (saturated KNO₃ gel) completes the circuit internally. Read the analogue needle first, then use "Show reading" to check yourself.
