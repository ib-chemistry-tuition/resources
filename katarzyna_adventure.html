Route and town positions are illustrative, not precise GPS: town markers are placed by interpolating each town's real longitude along a straight Frankfurt–Warsaw line, so they sit near the likely corridor but aren't an actual recorded flight track. Poznań, in particular, lies a bit north of the direct line — this is flagged on the map. The 2026 jet duration (≈1h 50m) is the real scheduled flight time. The 1930s biplane duration (≈7h 40m) is an estimate based on typical cruise speeds of early Lufthansa aircraft (~150 km/h), assuming no refuelling stop. Your flight's start time and aircraft choice are saved, so reopening this page later will show the correct progress automatically.
